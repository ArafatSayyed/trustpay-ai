# TrustPay AI

> Intelligent Risk Decisions for Every Payment

## 1. Problem Statement

Payment-risk teams need a faster and more explainable way to identify and prioritize suspicious transactions.

Traditional risk systems can generate many alerts, but they may not clearly explain why a transaction is risky or what action should be taken.

This can make it difficult for risk analysts to quickly distinguish genuine transactions from suspicious activity while reducing false positives.

### Our Problem

> How might we help payment-risk teams identify suspicious transactions quickly while clearly explaining why a transaction is risky and what action should be taken?

## 2. Design Thinking

### 2.1 Empathize

TrustPay AI focuses mainly on payment-risk teams and risk analysts who investigate suspicious transactions.

#### Primary User — Risk Analyst

Risk analysts need to:

- Identify suspicious transactions
- Investigate risky payments
- Understand why a transaction is risky
- Prioritize high-risk cases
- Make quick Allow, Review, or Block decisions
- Reduce false positives

#### Other Stakeholders

**Merchants**
- Reduce payment fraud
- Reduce false declines
- Protect revenue
- Maintain smooth payment experiences

**Customers**
- Make fast and secure payments
- Avoid unnecessary payment declines
- Understand why verification is required
- Resolve suspicious-payment situations easily

### Main Pain Points

Risk analysts may face:

- Large numbers of transaction alerts
- Difficulty prioritizing important cases
- Risk scores without clear explanations
- False positives affecting legitimate transactions
- Multiple signals that need to be checked manually
- Difficulty making quick decisions when information is scattered

### Key Empathy Insight

The core challenge is not simply detecting suspicious transactions.

Risk analysts need to understand:

- **Why** a transaction is risky
- **Which** cases matter most
- **What** action should be considered

> **Risk teams need clear, prioritized, and explainable risk information to make faster and more trustworthy decisions.**
