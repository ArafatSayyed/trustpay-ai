#TrustPay

>Intelligent Risk Decisions for Every Payment

## Probleam Statement

Payment risk teams need a faster and more explainable way to identify and prioritize suspicious transactions.

Traditional risk systems can generate large numbers of alerts, but they may not clearly ezplain why a transaction is considered risky or what action should be taken.

This can make it difficult for eisk analysts to quickly distinguish genuine transactions from suspicious activity while minimizing false positives.

### Our Problem

How might we help payment risk team identify suspicious transactions quickly while clearly explaining why a transaction is risky and what action should be taken ?
